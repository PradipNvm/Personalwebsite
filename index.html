About Me

Hello! I'm Pradip Pokhrel, a passionate 3D Generalist with a B.Sc. in Animation and 2-3 years of hands-on industry experience. My journey in the world of 3D art started with a fascination for bringing digital worlds to life, and it has evolved into a career where I blend creativity with technical expertise.

As a generalist with an upper edge in modeling, I've developed a comprehensive skill set that allows me to handle various aspects of 3D production. From detailed character modeling with proper topology flow to product visualization and animation, I enjoy the challenge of creating assets that are both visually stunning and technically sound.

What sets my work apart is my ability to integrate AI technologies into traditional 3D workflows, creating innovative solutions for product promotion and visualization. I'm also intrigued by the intersection of creativity and finance, which gives me a unique perspective on commercial 3D work.
